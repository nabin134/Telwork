Safety

Your privacy and safety come first.

Never share financial information or send money to anyone you meet online. Keep personal details private until you trust someone, and report any behavior that makes you uncomfortable — our team acts on every report.

  • Meet in public places for the first few times
  • Tell a friend where you are going
  • Do not share OTPs, bank details, or passwords
  • Use Telegram support if something feels wrong
